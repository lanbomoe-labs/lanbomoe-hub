Frequently Asked Questions

What is LanBo-Moe?
LanBo-Moe is a purpose-driven global brand founded by Moses Olanrewaju Bolarin that transforms lives through faith, creativity, innovation, and digital empowerment.

LanBo-Moe serves as an ecosystem connecting multimedia education, AI training, publishing, creative services, and mentorship. Through its sub-brands — 3niti Multimedia Academy (3MA), Majestic World of Business (MWB), Majestic Multimedia Academy (MMA), and others — LanBo-Moe equips individuals, students, entrepreneurs, and organizations with future-ready skills for a rapidly changing world.

Who is Moses Olanrewaju Bolarin?
Moses Olanrewaju Bolarin (LanBo-Moe) is a digital transformation strategist, bestselling author of over 15 books, worship leader with 100+ songs, and founder of the LanBo-Moe ecosystem.

With a Computer Science degree from the University of Lagos and a Post Graduate Diploma in Public Relations, Moses has over 27 years of experience in brand, IT, multimedia, and global strategy. He was ranked in the top 6% of Nigerian executives in 2015 and received the prestigious "Award of Excellence". Saved in 1986 while in high school, he has dedicated his life to ministry and entrepreneurship.

What is 3niti Multimedia Academy (3MA)?
3niti Multimedia Academy (3MA) is the flagship educational arm of LanBo-Moe, empowering kids and teens ages 4-19 with digital creativity and Kingdom values.

3MA offers hands-on courses in multimedia production, coding, AI, game design, robotics, virtual/augmented reality, and more. Programs are delivered online via Google Classroom and Zoom, and onsite at locations in Lagos and Ogun State, Nigeria. Students become "published authors" and skilled creators through the academy's unique project-based approach.

What is Summer Multimedia Training (SMT)?
SMT is an intensive annual summer program running from August 3-28, 2026, offering hands-on training in AI, coding, game design, robotics, and multimedia production.

SMT 2026 features modules MP1-MP7 covering text, images, audio, video, animations, presentations, and mobile content. The program is offered both online (via Google Classroom and Zoom) and onsite at Ikeja HQ. Students gain practical, future-ready skills through project-based learning and receive certificates upon completion.

What is Majestic World of Business (MWB)?
Majestic World of Business (MWB) is the parent creative enterprise behind LanBo-Moe's publishing, production, and consulting efforts.

MWB encompasses MWB Publishing (book publishing and author services), MWB Productions (music and video spanning global standards), and Global Strategy & Consulting (brand development, IT solutions, digital transformation, and global marketing).

What is Mobile University?
Mobile University is LanBo-Moe's unified platform for all educational and training programs.

Mobile University brings together 3MA (kids and teens), MMA (adults 19+), AVIS AI Video Training, SMT, CBT, and all other training offerings under one mobile-first, accessible platform. Students can access courses, take assessments, and earn certifications from anywhere.
